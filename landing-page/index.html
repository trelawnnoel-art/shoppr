SHOPPR
In development

Shop any store through a real person, in real time.

SHOPPR connects customers to local gig-worker "Scouts" who shop in-store on their behalf — picking items, confirming sizes and fit, and delivering same day. Customers describe what they need, get matched to nearby stores, and track their Scout from pickup to delivery.

✨

AI-matched shopping

Describe what you need in plain language — SHOPPR AI matches it to real, in-stock items at nearby stores.

🧍

3D avatar fitting

Customers build a 3D avatar — sized to their height and build — to preview how clothing from different stores will actually fit before a Scout buys it.

📦

Live Scout tracking

Follow a Mission from "Scout accepted" to "delivered," with direct chat to your Scout the whole way.

Actively building — core shopping flow, AI search, and Scout tracking are functional in our working prototype.
